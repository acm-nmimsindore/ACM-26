MEMBERSHIP FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Membership is open to all students at NMIMS Indore across engineering, computer science, data science, and technology disciplines — regardless of their current coding experience level.
Members gain full access to the ACM Digital Library, exclusive priority entry to HackACM & CodeSprint, personalized mentorship circles, discounts on international tech summits, and official ACM chapter certificates.
ACM-W NMIMS Indore operates as a separate dedicated chapter with its own independent website, events, and leadership team. Click below to open the official ACM-W portal.
Visit ACM-W Website
Core committee recruitment drives are held at the beginning of each academic semester. Applications open across Technical, Events, PR/Marketing, Design, and Logistics domains. Follow our Instagram for announcements.
Joining the student chapter is free. Optional paid ACM global membership ($19/year for students) unlocks the ACM Digital Library and conference discounts. Check our join page for current details.
Follow @acm_nmims_indore on Instagram, join our WhatsApp announcements group (link on the join page), or check the Events section on this website. All major events are posted 2–4 weeks in advance.
