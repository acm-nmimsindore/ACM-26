01 / Chapter

About ACM NMIMS Indore

The chapter at NMIMS Indore

The official student chapter of the world's largest educational and scientific computing society — powering hackathons, AI research, cybersecurity, and open-source innovation at NMIMS Indore.

02 / Strategic pillars

How we build tech leaders

Coding Culture

24-hour hackathons, competitive programming sprints, and system design labs to sharpen practical engineering skills.

Community & Inclusion

An inclusive developer ecosystem bringing together builders across all engineering domains, skill levels, and years.

Global Research

Direct access to ACM's international Digital Library, research journals, and global expert keynote speakers.

Career Launchpad

Mock technical interviews, alumni sessions, and direct referral opportunities for chapter members.

03 / Chapter journey

Milestones & roadmap

Q3 2024 / Inception

Chapter Charter

Founded at NMIMS Indore with 50 student developers and 4 faculty mentors. Received official ACM charter recognition globally.

Q4 2024 / HackACM 2.0

Smart Campus Hackathon

120+ hackers, 26 teams, and live industry mentorship sessions over 24 hours. ₹50,000+ in prizes were distributed.

Q1 2025 / Growth

500 Members Milestone

Crossed 500 active members and launched 8 specialized technical domains with dedicated domain leads and mentorship.

Q3 2025 / ACM-W Charter

Women in Computing

Official launch of the ACM-W sub-chapter with 40+ female student leaders. The first mentorship cohort of 80+ members completed.

Q4 2025 / CodeSprint

National Level Contest

CodeSprint expanded nationally with 300+ participants from 15+ colleges. National coverage earned chapter recognition.

2026 / Next chapter

AI Symposium & Grants

Expanding research, national hackathons, and competing for global ACM chapter excellence and international grants.
